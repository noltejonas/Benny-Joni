Train against your friends – with one shared weekly goal.

Benny & Joni turns “we really should work out more” into a challenge that sticks. Agree how many push-ups, pull-ups or squats you’ll do together this week. Everyone has their share, and every set counts straight away, for everyone to see.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a challenge and invite friends with a code or link
• Set a weekly goal: one or more exercises, you choose the amount
• Log sets in seconds, even after the fact
• See who’s delivering in the live feed and react with emojis
• Stats and duel view: who’s ahead, who has the longest streak?

FOR EVERY GROUP
• 1 v 1, 2 v 2 or everyone against everyone
• Your own exercises and set workouts with a timer
• Several challenges at once, for example with your mates and with your family

PENALTY POT, IF YOU WANT
Miss your share and you pay into your group’s kitty. The app only keeps the list – no money goes through the app. You can switch the penalty pot on or off for each challenge.

MOTIVATION WITHOUT THE NAGGING
• At most one notification per day and challenge, for example when someone takes the lead
• No ads, no tracking
• No chat and no phone number needed: only your group sees your sets

Want a look first? “Try the demo” works without an account.

In English and German – the app follows your iPhone’s language.
