Train against your friends – with one shared weekly goal.

Benny & Joni turns “we should work out more” into your next challenge. Agree how many push-ups, pull-ups or squats you will do together this week. Everyone has their share. Every set you log makes your progress visible.

YOUR GOAL, YOUR CHALLENGE
• Invite friends to a challenge with a code or link
• Play 1 v 1, 2 v 2 or everyone against everyone
• Set one or more weekly goals with your chosen exercises
• Log sets quickly and add entries you forgot
• Use your own exercises and workouts with a timer
• Set multiple Bring Sally Up runs per person per week
• Take part in several challenges at once

SEE WHO KEEPS GOING
Follow your group's sets in the live feed and react with emojis. Statistics show your progress and direct comparisons. The leaderboard lets you compete with others every week and collect medals. You choose in settings whether your own results appear publicly.

AN OPTIONAL PENALTY POT
Want to agree an amount for your group kitty when someone misses a goal? The optional penalty pot keeps your list. The app does not process, hold or transfer money and does not award cash prizes.

TAKE A LOOK FIRST
The demo shows the app with sample data, without creating an account. Register with email and password to start your own challenges.

Choose English, German, Finnish or Swedish in the app. You can turn off notifications and playful kicks from your training partners in settings.

Questions and feedback can be sent through the support page.
