• 36 standard exercises: from diamond push-ups to muscle-ups, leg curls and pistol squats, each with a short how-to and an animated figure. All of them count for the leaderboard.
• New exercise picker with search and groups (push, pull, legs, core …). Type “Squats” and you get the standard squats, so everyone logs the same exercise.
• Week trailer: with three or more challenges, the week finale plays as one clip with your record and some cheeky lines.
• Home screen widget (iOS 17 and later) with your week and open exercises.
• “All challenges”: Today, Feed, Stats and Penalty pot across all your challenges at a glance.
• “Start the new week”: restart all challenges with last week’s goals, optionally +5% or +10%.
• The leaderboard now counts reps instead of points.
• Invites are a single link that also works in WhatsApp.
